2. Architectural principles that emerged

Port the method, never the machinery
Kept: the 4-axis voice framework (reference/personality-layer.md), derivation-as-conversation, conflict rules, the transformation matrix. Left: the 249-note methodology graph, vault three-space architecture, 16 note skills, the numeric confidence scoring (weighted signals, 1.5 resolution threshold).
The scoring machinery exists to stop an interview across 8 dimensions feeding 15 generated files; a persona is one cheap human-reviewed file. Rigor machinery must match blast radius.
A persona file is a declarative, role-agnostic sheet
Rendering is a function of persona x role (creator, audience, reviewer, agent identity - open set) x consumer, owned by the consumer via the transformation matrix. Personas never embed prompts or roles; the kind field is an affordance hint, not a role lock.
Surfaced 2026-09-14 when Jeremy generalized "audience pack parallel to brand pack" to "creator is also parallel", exposing that the matrix as first written was one axis short.
Voice, not substance (composition rule)
Personas carry stance and voice; skills carry methodology; global principles carry standing constraints. A persona never overrides a rule - substance beats voice, explicit beats implicit. Provenance stack: harness, then global principles, then skill, then persona; spawned agents are derived renderings, regenerated, never hand-forked.
Durable invariants are how a lean v1 avoids costing future scale
Stable names as identifiers; self-contained portable text (model- and harness-agnostic); compact sheets (step-0 loading); provenance metadata (author, owner, created, last-validated, derivation source); trust boundary (stance and voice only, never permissions or tool instructions, treated as untrusted input).
Missed scale never comes from building too little in v1; it comes from format decisions that foreclose unmapped futures. Divergence feeds invariants, invariants constrain seam work, v1 stays thin.
Three qualitative bins replace numeric confidence
persona-new's single hybrid flow labels each extracted trait stated / inferred / defaulted; the confirm pass covers only the weak bins. Binary stated/not-stated loses the inferred middle and either ships wrong extractions silently or re-asks what was communicated.

3. Design-process learnings

Brainstorm from consumers backward, not from source material forward
The session's biggest miss: anchoring on arscontexta's creation-engine shape produced "creation + a thin apply" while Jeremy, thinking from usage backward, supplied the four-command surface, the two-persona composition (voice x audience), and the role axis.
Diagnosed on Jeremy's direct ask "what had you as the brainstormer miss these possibilities?"; root causes: source-forward anchoring, unary application model, convergence pressure applied during divergence, sweeping tools instead of the workflow behind them. Saved to persistent memory as brainstorm-from-consumers-backward.
The possibility sweep (4 moves) closes that gap structurally
Future-back pass, cross-product enumeration, consumer-backward walk, scale probes - run before convergence, output capped to parked items and durable invariants, banned from widening Done-looks-like. Filed as jrit-loop#17; dogfooded on this brief, yielding 4 invariants and 4 parked items.
Near-miss: a proposal quietly reversing a settled decision
Jeremy's four-command proposal said personas are "saved to your personal cross-project library", which would have silently dropped the settled global+project two-home answer. Flagged out loud; Jeremy kept global+project. Supersessions must be explicit decisions with dates - the brief records the one real supersession ("both, layered" replaced by the single hybrid flow, 2026-09-14).
Near-miss: verb collision in the command surface
persona-new (makes a persona) vs persona-create (makes content) would be misremembered constantly. Renamed; "draft" was rejected because deliverables span writing, design, and diagrams - persona-craft chosen, aligning with the future craft skillpack.
Adjudicating another session's proposal: agree only after an adversarial pass
Another session's hybrid persona-new recommendation was verified against disk (the 1.5 threshold claim checked in skills/setup/SKILL.md lines 185-340), agreed with in core, and amended twice: three-bin labels instead of binary, and the silent supersession of "both, layered" surfaced as an explicit call.
Fresh-context review finds what the author's chair cannot
The Rubix pass (Lens A impacted-professional, Lens B cold craft on Opus) found: the port's source of record in wipeable /tmp, an unverified eval-harness capability underwriting two [executed-check] criteria, missing MIT attribution, and the persona-as-injection-surface trust gap. None had surfaced inline.

Jeremy asked for "4D thinking" and future-back work that "should never cost me missed scale, power, or impact" - satisfied via the parked-items-plus-invariants cap, not scope growth.
