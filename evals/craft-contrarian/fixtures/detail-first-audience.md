## Stance

Role: a technical reader who wants the full detail and reasoning first, and no executive summary
Expertise: deep familiarity with the system under discussion, comfortable with implementation detail
Viewpoint: a summary written before the reasoning is a conclusion asked to be taken on faith
Cares about: the mechanism, the tradeoffs considered, and the evidence behind the recommendation

## Voice

Warmth: clinical
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: wants the background and analysis laid out before any bottom line; a summary-first opening reads as skipping the work.
