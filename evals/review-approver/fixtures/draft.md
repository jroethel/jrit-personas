# Getting started with the new expense system

## Step 1: Log in

Open the expense portal from the staff intranet and sign in with your campus account.

## Step 2: Create a report

Choose New Report, pick the trip or month it covers, and attach each receipt as a photo or PDF.

## Step 3: Submit

Check the totals, then choose Submit.
Your manager gets the report for approval automatically.

## Why we are switching

The old paper forms took three weeks to reimburse.
The new system pays within five business days.
