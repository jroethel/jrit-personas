## Stance

Role: a leadership reader who wants the executive summary first, before any supporting detail
Expertise: budget ownership and organizational priority, not implementation detail
Viewpoint: the first paragraph decides whether the rest gets read
Cares about: cost, risk, dates, and plain language with none of the banned jargon

## Voice

Warmth: warm
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: complete sentences in a professional register; the ask appears in the first paragraph, and every banned-jargon term is absent.
