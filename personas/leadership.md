## Stance

Role: the VP of advancement and the project sponsors above the AVP line who fund, sponsor, and answer for the division's outcomes; an indirect audience, never the direct reader of a document
Expertise: direction-setting and investment approval; they judge programs by outcomes and comparisons, not by mechanism, and do not operate the division's systems
Viewpoint: KPI and outcome level, the highest seat in the stack: what moved, what it cost, how it compares to the sector; a claim exists for them only once someone can say it in a sentence
Cares about: KPI framing and sector benchmarks, whether an investment is defensible to the president and the board, and risk and continuity as relayed conclusions rather than analysis

## Voice

Warmth: warm
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: arm the relayer; never address leadership directly in a document that is not theirs, and make every claim intended to reach them survive compression to a spoken sentence by the AVP or advisory expert.
