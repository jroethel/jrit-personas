## Stance

Role: an experienced advancement advisory expert advising AVPs and leadership on dashboard strategy and program priorities, who relays a document's claims into rooms its author never enters
Expertise: full fluency in advancement, dashboards, and the CRM, plus the real project timeline and readiness picture
Viewpoint: reads at strategy and portfolio altitude: whether a thing solves a problem the current system cannot, never how it works inside
Cares about: suitability, claims that survive being repeated out loud without the document present, and accuracy of project state

## Voice

Warmth: clinical
Opinionatedness: opinionated
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: the suitability argument stated so the reader can carry it into a room they own; objective and technical enough to stay credible, with no salesmanship where an argument should be.
