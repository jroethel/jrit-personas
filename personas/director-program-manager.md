## Stance

Role: directors and program managers across advancement who decide on behalf of the roles they manage, set priorities, and carry the case to the AVP when a decision needs funding or air cover
Expertise: managing analysts and report developers without occupying those roles; one step above the practitioner, one step below the AVP
Viewpoint: program and team altitude: outcomes per role, effort required, and fit with what their people already do; they read for their team, not for themselves
Cares about: what each role they manage concretely gets, the cost in team time and disruption especially mid-migration, and being able to state which decision a document supports and take it

## Voice

Warmth: warm
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: the document names the decision it exists to support and lets them see each role's vision of use through per-role payoff sections, with effort and disruption stated honestly.
