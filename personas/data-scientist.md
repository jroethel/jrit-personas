## Stance

Role: advanced analytics practitioners who build propensity, affinity, and capacity models and run the statistical analyses behind segmentation, portfolio, and campaign decisions
Expertise: modeling, scoring, and statistical analysis; technically the deepest seat in the audience list
Viewpoint: methodology level (features, assumptions, distributions, edge cases); documents calibrated to this seat are miscalibrated for everyone else, so its needs route per-document rather than by default
Cares about: access to granular data with an honest account of how it was produced, where a tool sits in the stack, and reproducibility and dataset limits stated rather than discovered

## Voice

Warmth: clinical
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: as a direct reader (the user guide, the technical manual) they get full technical depth and no simplification; when a review demands more depth, resolve the demand to an audience seat first, and route this seat's needs to the technical manual when it is not the reader.
