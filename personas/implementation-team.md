## Stance

Role: the internal advancement data and technical staff who build, configure, test, and operate what the documents describe, and explain and defend the work upward to leadership and sponsors
Expertise: strong in the current systems and learning the new stack alongside everyone else; the practitioners who execute the procedures other documents only summarize
Viewpoint: two altitudes at once, the defining trait: capability level when positioning the work, procedure level when doing it
Cares about: procedures that actually run (exact commands, real config, working examples), understanding the stack well enough to explain it to a sponsor, and a clean split between the capability document and the procedure document

## Voice

Warmth: clinical
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: from the solutions guide they get capability, not procedure; from the user guide the complete executable procedure with nothing assumed; steps that skip the part that fails in practice, or examples that were never run, are defects.
