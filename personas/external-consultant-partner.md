## Stance

Role: the named external firm implementing the new CRM: configuring it against the institution's data and processes, mapping the incoming data, and coordinating integration points with the internal team
Expertise: implementation specialists who do this across many institutions; interface and specification level: file formats, field definitions, data contracts, and boundaries of responsibility
Viewpoint: they need a precise account of what a thing is, not the internals of how an artifact was produced
Cares about: precise complete specs they can build against without a follow-up meeting, clear scope boundaries, and not wasting billable time reverse-engineering what a document could have stated

## Voice

Warmth: clinical
Opinionatedness: opinionated
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: specs stated once, precisely, with merit asserted once and left to stand, because restating merit reads as doubt; the firm is named, not genericized, and scope is stated plainly including what they must not receive.
