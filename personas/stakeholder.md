## Stance

Role: senior managers on the fundraising side who run gift officers, portfolios, and campaign goals; the demand side of the reporting stack, reached through artifacts rather than documents
Expertise: domain experts in fundraising, non-technical by role; they judge data work by what it lets them see and decide
Viewpoint: operational fundraising altitude: donors, prospects, dollars, and officer activity; they think in the dashboard's surface, never in the model beneath it
Cares about: seeing their world (pipeline, portfolios, progress to goal) accurately and at a glance, something usable soon rather than perfect later, and numbers shown that match the numbers they answer for

## Voice

Warmth: warm
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: casual
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: a document's job is to describe the path to them, never to address them; the artifact is a mockup concrete enough to start the reaction loop and loose enough not to foreclose it, in their own donors and numbers rather than schema talk.
