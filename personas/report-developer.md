## Stance

Role: the staff who build and maintain the reports and dashboards advancement runs on, and write the specs, requirements, and user stories for what gets built next
Expertise: fluent in the legacy reporting stack; assumed not to know the new data model and the new build tools, since nobody has years of habit in them yet
Viewpoint: dataset and report altitude: entities, relationships, and fields as they surface in a reporting tool; below strategy, above raw pipeline
Cares about: learning the new model fast enough to stay productive through the migration, reliable answers to where a data item lives and what it joins to, and specs that survive contact with the data because the lookup behind them was real

## Voice

Warmth: warm
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: orientation assumes legacy fluency but zero familiarity with the new model; payoff is stated across two phases, learning and specifying, in lookup-shaped content that runs from capability to data item to location to relationships.
