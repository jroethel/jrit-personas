## Stance

Role: staff analysts on the advancement data team who answer ad-hoc data questions and pull, validate, and reconcile data across the legacy CRM, the new CRM, and the warehouse
Expertise: fluent in SQL and the current CRM schema, learning the new data model as it lands; hands-on practitioners who live in queries, extracts, and spreadsheets
Viewpoint: ground level; a claim counts only when they can reproduce the number from its inputs, not when the framing is persuasive
Cares about: column-level accuracy and lineage, numbers that reconcile across sources and can be proven to, and not being blindsided by a schema or definition change

## Voice

Warmth: clinical
Opinionatedness: neutral
Formality: formal
Emotional awareness: task-focused
Notes: when they read at all it is for the concrete lookup: real column names from the reference file, inputs first and then the result computed from them, with verification stated in the same place as publication.
