Trigger: after the persona-seats run landed on `persona-seats` (unpushed), Jeremy asked why `seats` is required when the `## As <seat>` sections are optional, and asked for the written rationale before changing anything.

## Problem

The persona-seats run replaced `kind` with a required `seats` list, and three skills now refuse a persona that lacks the seat they want: craft refuses a voice without `creator` or a reader without `audience`, review refuses a mode whose seat is missing, spawn refuses a persona without `creator`.
That turned the list into a role lock, which the 2026-09-14 port brief had ruled out: "The kind field is an affordance hint, not a role lock."
The lock carries no judgment: 11 of 14 shipped sheets say `seats: [audience]` only because `sed` copied the old `kind: audience`, so today `leadership` cannot be a voice and `jeremy-voice` cannot be a reader even though Stance and Voice are all either role needs.
The two protections the list stood in for are richness before writing in a voice and consent to write as a real person, and the list measures neither.
Richness is handled here by spawn's provenance disclosure.
Consent to write as a real person stays parked as issue #7: every sheet in the library is owned by Jeremy, so an owner-keyed guard would fire on every voice persona, and the right trigger (who the sheet depicts, not who maintains it) belongs to the team-use design.

## Design

### Seats are derived

A persona is one person and may sit in any seat: creator (writes in this voice), reviewer (approves other people's material), audience (reads material shaped for it).
Stance and Voice are shared across every seat and are always enough to sit in it.
A `## As <seat>` section is optional and additive: when the persona sits in that seat and the section exists, the section applies on top of Stance and Voice; when it does not exist, Stance and Voice alone apply.
The `seats` frontmatter key is retired.
The format check rejects a `seats:` line exactly as it rejects `kind:`, with a one-line fix: delete the line.
`## As <seat>` section names stay restricted to `creator`, `reviewer`, `audience`, each section still holds only its own labels, and the per-section label rules are unchanged.
`jargon-tolerance`, `banned-jargon`, and `time-budget` are allowed on any persona and are read only when the persona sits as audience; the "audience seat only" wording and the checker rule that rejects them go.

### Skills

- craft: takes any persona as voice and any persona as audience, the same persona may be both; applies `## As creator` and `## As audience` when present exactly as the seats brief specified; drops the seat refusal and the "no fallback" sentence; the legacy stop covers both `kind:` and `seats:` lines with the delete-the-line fix.
- review: picks the mode from the request as the seats brief specified; drops the missing-seat stop; approver mode reads `## As reviewer` when present and otherwise reviews against the Stance `Cares about:` with the existing absent-section note.
- spawn: drops the creator refusal; before writing the agent file it states the persona's `derivation-source` and `last-validated` so thinness is visible at the moment of spawning; it still renders `## As creator` lines when present and screens them like `Notes:`.
- new: no longer asks which seats apply; it asks which `## As <seat>` sections have items worth writing and writes only those; its self-check drops the `seats` items and keeps the section rules.

## Rejected alternatives

- Keep the required list as an explicit permission to write as the persona: it is a label set by migration, not a judgment, and it contradicts the hint-not-lock invariant.
- Make `seats` optional, absent meaning any seat: two semantics for one key.
- A richness gate before spawning: not well defined; provenance disclosure gives the human the signal instead.
- A real-person confirmation guard keyed on `owner` (issue #7): considered on 2026-10-08 and parked again, because every sheet is owned by Jeremy and the field names the maintainer, not the person depicted; it fired on every voice persona and would have needed a pre-confirmation sentence in five eval prompts.
